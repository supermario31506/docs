Trading SILVER stock is the core gameplay loop of Mario Nintendo. You can buy shares when you expect the price to rise and sell them later at a profit — or cut your losses if the market moves against you. Every trade executes instantly at the **current live price**, so timing matters.

## Buying a Share

To buy one share of SILVER stock, click the green **BUY** button on the right-side panel or press **B** on your keyboard.

When a buy order is executed:

1. The **current live price** is deducted from your cash balance.
2. Your **share count increases by 1**.
3. Your **net worth** updates immediately to reflect the new position.

There is no partial buying — each action purchases exactly **one share** at a time.

### Buy Requirements

You must have **enough cash on hand** to cover the current price. If your cash balance is less than the live price of SILVER, the buy action will silently fail and no transaction will take place. Always check your cash display in the header before placing a buy order.

<Warning>
Spending all your cash on shares leaves you unable to buy more if the price drops further, removing the opportunity to average down. Keep a reserve in mind so you're never completely locked out of future trades.
</Warning>

## Selling a Share

To sell one share, click the red **SELL** button on the right-side panel or press **S** on your keyboard.

When a sell order is executed:

1. The **current live price** is added to your cash balance.
2. Your **share count decreases by 1**.
3. Your **net worth** updates immediately.

Like buying, each action sells exactly **one share** at a time.

### Sell Requirements

You must hold **at least 1 share** to sell. If your share count is zero, the sell action will silently fail. You cannot short-sell or go into a negative share position.

## Profit and Loss Example

Here is a simple example of a profitable trade sequence:

| Action | Price | Cash Change | Shares |
|--------|-------|-------------|--------|
| Starting state | — | $250.00 | 1 |
| Buy 1 share | $25.00 | −$25.00 → $225.00 | 2 |
| Price rises to $40 | — | (no action) | 2 |
| Sell 1 share | $40.00 | +$40.00 → $265.00 | 1 |
| **Profit on that share** | | **+$15.00** | |

In this example, buying one share at $25 and selling it at $40 yields a **$15 profit** per share. The same logic applies in reverse — if you buy at $40 and sell at $25, you incur a **$15 loss**.

## Mouse vs. Keyboard

Both input methods trigger the exact same trade logic:

| Method | Buy | Sell |
|--------|-----|------|
| Mouse click | Green **BUY** button | Red **SELL** button |
| Keyboard | **B** key | **S** key |

<Tip>
Watch the candlestick chart for a series of red (down) blocks followed by a potential reversal before buying. Purchasing shares during a price dip and selling during a subsequent rally is the simplest way to generate profit in Mario Nintendo.
</Tip>
