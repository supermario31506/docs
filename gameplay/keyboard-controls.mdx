Mario Nintendo supports keyboard shortcuts for every major action in the game. Whether you prefer clicking the on-screen buttons or keeping your hands on the keyboard, both input methods trigger the exact same underlying trade logic with no difference in execution speed or price.

## Keyboard Shortcut Reference

| Key | Action | Description |
|-----|--------|-------------|
| **B** | Buy 1 Share | Purchases one share of SILVER stock at the current live price. Requires sufficient cash. |
| **S** | Sell 1 Share | Sells one share of SILVER stock at the current live price. Requires at least 1 share held. |
| **Q** | Quit / Stop Simulation | Immediately halts the price simulation and freezes the chart. |

These shortcuts are also listed in the **bottom bar** of the game window for easy reference: *"Click Buttons or press [B] Buy | [S] Sell | [Q] Quit"*.

## Stopping the Simulation (Q)

Pressing **Q** stops the price simulation entirely. When triggered:

- The live price feed halts and no new candlestick blocks will be generated.
- The chart freezes in place, preserving the last visible state.
- A **"SIMULATION STOPPED (Press Refresh to Restart)"** overlay appears on screen to confirm the simulation is no longer running.

No further buy or sell orders can be placed after the simulation is stopped. To start a new session, **refresh your browser** — this resets everything back to the starting state ($250.00 cash, 1 share, price $25.00).

## Mouse Button Equivalents

If you prefer using the mouse, the on-screen buttons in the right-side panel perform exactly the same actions:

| Mouse Target | Equivalent Key | Action |
|--------------|----------------|--------|
| Green **BUY** button | **B** | Buy 1 share at current price |
| Red **SELL** button | **S** | Sell 1 share at current price |

There is no mouse equivalent for the **Q** (quit) key — stopping the simulation is keyboard-only.

<Tip>
Keyboard shortcuts shine during fast-moving markets. When you spot a sudden price dip or spike on the chart, tapping **B** or **S** is significantly quicker than moving your mouse to the button panel and clicking — giving you a better chance of executing at your target price before the next block closes.
</Tip>
