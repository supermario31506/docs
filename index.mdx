## Get Started

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the game">
    Open `main.html` in any modern browser — no install, no login required.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Watch the chart">
    The SILVER stock price updates every second. Green blocks mean the price rose; red blocks mean it fell.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Buy your first share">
    Click the **BUY** button or press **B** on your keyboard to buy one share at the current market price.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sell for a profit">
    When the price rises, click **SELL** or press **S** to sell and lock in your gains.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  You start with **$250.00 cash** and **1 share** of SILVER at a starting price of **$25.00**. Your net worth is shown in real time at the top of the screen.
</Note>
