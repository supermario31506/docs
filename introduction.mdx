Mario Nintendo is a fully browser-based fake stock market simulator. You trade a single fictional asset — **SILVER stock** — watching its price move in real time on a live candlestick chart. There is no signup, no install, and no server: just open the HTML file and start trading.

## What Is Mario Nintendo?

Mario Nintendo puts you in the role of a solo trader with a starting portfolio of $250 cash and 1 share of SILVER stock. Your goal is simple: grow your net worth by buying shares when prices are low and selling when they are high.

The simulation runs entirely in your browser. Prices update every second and a new candlestick block forms every second, giving you a fast-moving chart to read and react to.

## Starting Conditions

| Setting | Value |
|---|---|
| Starting Cash | $250.00 |
| Starting Shares | 1 share of SILVER |
| Starting Price | $25.00 |
| Candlestick Interval | 1 second per block |

## How the Simulation Runs

Every frame, the simulation nudges the SILVER price up or down by a tiny random percentage. Occasionally, a rare random event causes a sudden **+$100 spike** or a **−$200 crash**, keeping the market unpredictable.

At the end of each 1-second interval, the current candlestick block is finalized and a new one begins. You can see the developing (active) block in real time as a hollow outline on the right side of the chart.

<Info>
  The price can never fall below **$1.00** — a built-in floor prevents the market from going to zero.
</Info>

## What You Can Do

- **Buy shares** — spend cash to acquire more SILVER at the current price
- **Sell shares** — convert shares back to cash at the current price
- **Watch the chart** — read candlestick history and the live price line
- **Track your portfolio** — see your cash, share count, and net worth at all times
